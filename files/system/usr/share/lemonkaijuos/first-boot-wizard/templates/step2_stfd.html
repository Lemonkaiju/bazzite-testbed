What is STFD?

STFD (Shut The Front Door) is a privacy-focused home network system that:

  • 🛡️ Blocks ads and trackers network-wide
  • 🔐 Provides secure remote access
  • 🤖 Manages family devices with AI assistance
  • 🏠 Keeps your data private (no cloud required)
